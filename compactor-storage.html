Compactor FAQ

Common Questions About Mobile Compactors

Find technical answers about our space-saving mobile storage systems.

How much floor space can a Mobile Compactor system save?

Our Mobile Compactor systems are designed to save up to 60-70% of floor space by eliminating multiple access aisles and utilizing a precision-engineered rail system that requires only one moving aisle.

How much does a mobile compactor system cost in India?

Mobile compactor system pricing depends on configuration, size, load capacity, and finish, so Space Planners provides a custom quote after a free site assessment rather than a fixed price list. Request a free assessment to get an accurate quote for your space.

What is the difference between a mobile compactor and a static rack?

A mobile compactor moves on rail tracks to eliminate multiple fixed access aisles, allowing storage density to increase by 60–70% compared to static racks, which each require their own permanent aisle. Static racks remain the better choice for very high-frequency, simultaneous multi-user access.

How long does it take to install a mobile compactor system?

Installation timelines depend on the size of the system and whether floor-recessed or surface-mounted rail tracks are used, but Space Planners' engineers are designed for minimal disruption to your existing facility and operations during setup.

Can mobile compactors be used in cold storage or temperature-controlled rooms?

Yes, mobile compactor systems can be customized with stainless steel components and vibration-free motorized movement for cold storage and pharmaceutical environments requiring strict temperature and hygiene control.

Are the compactors safe for delicate archival or medical materials?

Yes, they feature anti-tamper locks, vibration-free movement, and can be customized with perforated panels for ventilation or stainless steel components for medical storage.

What is the difference between manual and motorized compactors?

Manual compactors use an ergonomic hand-crank system for effortless movement, while motorized compactors offer push-button or remote access, ideal for high-frequency retrieval and large-scale industrial operations.

Is the rail system difficult to install on existing floors?

Our engineers specialize in seamless installation. We offer both floor-recessed tracks for a flush finish and surface-mounted tracks with ramps that integrate perfectly with your existing floor layout with minimal disruption.
