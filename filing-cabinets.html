Filing FAQ

Common Questions About Filing Cabinets

Find answers about our organized document management systems.

Do your filing cabinets accommodate both vertical and lateral filing?

Yes, our cabinets are available in vertical, lateral, and multi-drawer configurations to suit any document management workflow and office space requirement.

What is the difference between vertical and lateral filing cabinets?

Vertical filing cabinets store files front-to-back in narrow drawers and suit smaller footprints, while lateral filing cabinets store files side-to-side in wider drawers, offering easier browsing and higher document density per square foot. Space Planners manufactures both configurations.

Are fire-resistant filing cabinets required for government records in India?

While not universally mandated, many government departments and institutions prefer fire-resistant, double-walled filing cabinets for critical and irreplaceable records. Space Planners offers specialized fire-resistant units for this purpose.

Can filing cabinets be purchased through government e-procurement (GeM)?

Yes, many of Space Planners' filing cabinet and cupboard products are listed on the GeM (Government e-Marketplace) portal, enabling direct procurement by government departments and public sector undertakings.

How many drawers can a filing tower have?

Space Planners offers standard 2, 3, and 4-drawer filing units, with custom high-density towers manufactured up to 6 drawers high for offices needing to maximize vertical filing space.

Are the filing cabinets listed on the GeM portal?

Yes, many of our professional filing products are GeM-listed, making them easily accessible for government departments and public sector undertakings across India.

Do you offer fire-resistant storage for important documents?

We provide specialized double-walled filing cabinets that offer enhanced fire resistance for critical records, sensitive departmental archives, and vital corporate documents.

What is the maximum height for your filing towers?

We offer standard 2, 3, and 4-drawer units, but we can also manufacture custom high-density towers up to 6 drawers high to maximize vertical space in compact offices.
