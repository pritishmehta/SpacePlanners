Export FAQ

Global Export Questions

Answers for international procurement managers and contractors.

What is your typical shipping and lead time for export orders?

Manufacturing takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on order size and customization. Transit from JNPT (Mumbai) is typically 5 to 10 days to GCC ports (Jebel Ali, Sohar, Dammam), 14 to 21 days to East and South African ports (Durban, Mombasa) and 3 to 5 days to Sri Lanka.

Do you provide on-site installation supervision for overseas orders?

Yes. We supply installation manuals, marked assembly drawings and video walkthroughs. For larger turnkey projects we send Space Planners supervisors to train local labor and oversee the work.

What are your standard payment terms for international buyers?

Our standard terms are 30% advance by T/T on order confirmation and 70% against an irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C at sight) or against scanned Bill of Lading copies.

Can systems be customized for local voltage and environmental conditions?

Yes. Motorized compactor panels are configured to local single-phase or three-phase supply (220V / 380V / 415V, 50Hz or 60Hz). Stainless steel variants (SS 304 / SS 316) and epoxy powder coatings protect against corrosion in humid or cold-storage conditions (down to -20°C).
