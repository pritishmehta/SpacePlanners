Global Export Frequently Asked Questions

Essential answers for international procurement managers and project contractors.

What is your typical shipping and lead time for export orders?

Manufacturing lead time ranges between 2 to 4 weeks depending on order volume and custom specs. Transit times from JNPT Port (Mumbai) are typically 5–10 days to GCC ports (Jebel Ali, Sohar, Dammam), 14–21 days to East/South African ports (Durban, Mombasa), and 3–5 days to Sri Lanka.

Do you provide on-site installation supervision for overseas orders?

Yes. We offer complete installation manuals, marked component assembly drawings, and video walkthroughs. For large turnkey projects, we dispatch expert Space Planners installation supervisors overseas to train local labor and oversee execution.

What are your standard payment terms for international buyers?

Our standard export payment terms are 30% advance via T/T (Wire Transfer) upon order confirmation and 70% against irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C at sight) or against scanned Bill of Lading copies.

Can systems be customized for local voltage and environmental conditions?

Absolutely. Motorized compactor panels are configured to match local single-phase or three-phase voltages (220V/380V/415V, 50Hz/60Hz). Stainless steel variants (SS 304 / SS 316) and specialized epoxy powder coatings prevent corrosion in high-humidity or cold-storage environments (-20°C).
