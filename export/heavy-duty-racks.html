Heavy Duty & Pallet Racks Export FAQs

Technical questions on load capacities, seismic customization, and container freight.

What load capacities do your heavy duty racks support?

Our selective pallet racking supports beam capacities up to 4,000 kg per level, with frame capacities exceeding 24,000 kg per bay. Medium duty longspan shelving covers 300 kg to 800 kg per shelf.

Can racking systems be customized to local warehouse or seismic standards?

Yes. We supply custom base plates, heavy-duty expansion floor anchors, and seismic bracing configurations calculated to conform to local civil engineering codes across GCC, African, and Asian zones.

How are pallet racks packed for ocean container export?

Beams and frames are bundled with protective corrugated paper and heavy plastic wrapping. Small components (base plates, safety pins, anchor bolts) are packed into fumigated wooden crates designed to maximize 40ft High Cube container payload.
