Can racking systems be customized to local warehouse or seismic standards?

Yes. We supply custom base plates, heavy-duty expansion anchors and seismic bracing calculated to local civil engineering codes in GCC, African and Asian markets.

How are pallet racks packed for ocean container export?

Beams and frames are bundled with corrugated paper and heavy plastic wrap. Small parts (base plates, safety pins, anchor bolts) go into fumigated wooden crates, planned to maximise the payload of a 40 ft High Cube container.
