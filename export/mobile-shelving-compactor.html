Pharma & Cleanroom

GMP-Grade Stainless Steel Mobile Compactors

Built for overseas pharmaceutical plants, raw material stores and biological archives that need GMP compliance, no particle shedding and temperature-controlled operation.

  • SS 304 / SS 316 construction. Non-corrosive, non-magnetic brushed finish that is easy to sanitize. Suitable for Class 100 to Class 10,000 cleanrooms.

FAQ

Mobile Shelving & Compactor Questions

Can compactor systems be customized for GMP and pharma compliance?

Yes. We make SS 304 and SS 316 compactors without sharp crevices or particle-trapping edges, so they wash down cleanly and meet GMP cleanroom requirements.

Are systems suitable for cold storage or temperature-controlled environments?

Yes. Cold-room versions use low-temperature lubricants, stainless steel tracks, sealed bearings and cold-resistant polyurethane wheels, and operate down to -20°C.

What is your typical shipping and lead time for export compactors?

Orders dispatch in 2 to 3 weeks from CAD approval. Ocean transit to GCC ports takes 5 to 10 days and to African ports 14 to 21 days.
