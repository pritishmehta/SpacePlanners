Mobile Shelving & Compactor FAQs

Technical export questions answered.

Can compactor systems be customized for GMP and pharma compliance?

Yes. We manufacture SS 304 and SS 316 grade compactors designed without sharp crevices or particle-trapping edges, ensuring seamless wash-down and conformity with global GMP cleanroom standards.

Are systems suitable for cold storage or temperature-controlled environments?

Specifically engineered cold-room options feature specialized low-temperature lubricants, stainless steel tracks, sealed anti-friction bearings, and cold-resistant polyurethane drive wheels operational down to -20°C.

What is your typical shipping and lead time for export compactors?

Standard order dispatch is 2 to 3 weeks from receipt of CAD approval. Ocean shipping to GCC ports takes 5–10 days; African ports take 14–21 days.
