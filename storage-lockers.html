Locker FAQ

Common Questions About Storage Lockers

Find answers about our secure and customizable locker systems.

What locking options are available for your lockers?

We offer a wide variety of security options including traditional key locks,numeric locks, digital keypad locks, RFID systems, and advanced biometric access for high-security environments.

What is the difference between digital and biometric lockers?

Digital keypad lockers use a PIN code for access, while biometric lockers use fingerprint or similar identity verification for higher-security environments. Space Planners offers both, along with traditional key locks and RFID card access, depending on the security level required.

Are Space Planners' lockers suitable for gyms and fitness centers?

Yes, Space Planners manufactures rust-resistant, high-traffic-rated lockers suitable for gyms, factories, and offices, with a multi-stage anti-corrosion process and powder-coat finish designed for daily heavy use.

Can lockers be customized with company branding?

Yes, lockers can be finished in custom RAL powder-coated colors to match corporate branding, in addition to standard modular sizing or fully custom configurations.

Do hospital lockers need special certification in India?

Hospital and healthcare lockers typically need to meet healthcare alignment for infection control and durability. Space Planners manufactures healthcare-aligned lockers with antimicrobial coatings and integrated ventilation for sterile environments.

Are your lockers suitable for healthcare and hospital environments?

Yes, we manufacture healthcare-aligned lockers with antimicrobial coatings and integrated ventilation systems, specifically designed for staff and patient use in sterile hospital settings.

Can we customize the colors and sizes of the locker banks?

Definitely. We offer standard modular sizes as well as fully custom configurations. You can also choose from a wide range of RAL powder-coated colors to match your branding.

Are the lockers resistant to rust and heavy daily use?

Every locker is treated with a multi-stage anti-corrosion process and finished with high-quality powder coating to ensure long-term resistance to rust and wear in high-traffic areas.
