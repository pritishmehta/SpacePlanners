Racking FAQ

Common Questions About Industrial Racking

Find technical answers about our heavy-duty and modular racking solutions.

What is the load capacity of your heavy-duty racks?

Our industrial racking systems are rated to support loads up to 10 tons per level, depending on the configuration and specific steel grade used. We provide detailed load charts with every installation.

What types of industrial racking does Space Planners offer?

Space Planners offers heavy-duty pallet racks rated up to 10 tons per level, modular boltless racks for lighter storage, slotted angle racks, and structural or rack-supported mezzanine floor systems, all in galvanized steel for warehouse and industrial use.

Are Space Planners' racking systems suitable for e-commerce fulfillment centers?

Yes, boltless modular racks are designed for high picking efficiency in e-commerce fulfillment and retail distribution centers, offering easy reconfiguration as inventory and SKU counts change.

Do industrial racks need to be bolted to the floor?

It depends on the rack type. Heavy-duty pallet racks rated for multi-ton loads are typically anchored for stability and seismic safety, while boltless modular racks are designed for tool-free assembly and reconfiguration without floor fixing.

Can I expand my racking system later without relocating my warehouse?

Yes, Space Planners' racking systems are modular by design, meaning additional bays and tiers can be added later to expand storage capacity without needing to relocate or rebuild your existing warehouse layout.

Do you offer boltless racking for lighter storage?

Yes, we provide modular boltless racks which are easy to assemble and reconfigure, perfect for small parts storage, retail fulfillment, or e-commerce centers requiring high picking efficiency.

What safety features are included in your racking systems?

All our racks are OSHA-aligned, featuring safety pins, upright guards, and high-visibility finishes. We also ensure seismic stability and proper load labeling for every project.

Can you design multi-tier mezzanine floors?

Absolutely. We design and install structural and rack-supported mezzanine systems to effectively double or triple your vertical storage capacity, utilizing every cubic inch of your warehouse space.
